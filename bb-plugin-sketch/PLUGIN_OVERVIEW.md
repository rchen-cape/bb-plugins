A quiet, hand-drawn whiteboard for diagrams that sends what you draw to your Wiki.

## What you get

- A **Sketch** page in the sidebar: an infinite canvas with rectangles,
  diamonds, ellipses, arrows that attach to shapes, lines, pen strokes, text,
  and labels inside shapes, in Excalidraw's hand-drawn style.
- Excalidraw's keys and feel: one key per tool, drag a box to select, `⌘D`,
  `⌘G`, undo and redo, copy and paste (also to and from excalidraw.com), zoom
  and pan with the trackpad, and a grid.
- **Send to Wiki.** Select part of a sketch and send it as an image, either
  into a Wiki doc and heading you pick, or to the Wiki inbox to review like any
  other card (inbox cards need a recent Wiki).
- Light and dark themes that follow bb, with exports that always look right on
  white.
- `bb sketch` to list, create, export (SVG, PNG, `.excalidraw`), and send
  sketches, so agents can work with them too.

## Your files

Each sketch is a plain Excalidraw file in `~/Sketches`, saved as you draw. Open
them in excalidraw.com or any Excalidraw tool, and changes made there show up
in bb. If a file changes or disappears while you have unsaved edits, Sketch
asks what to do and never drops your edits; they can always be saved as a new
sketch.

## How it works

Everything runs locally: drawing, saving, and turning a selection into a PNG
all happen on your machine. Sending to the Wiki hands the image to the Wiki
plugin, which copies it into your Wiki folder or its inbox. Sketch needs no
account or API key.
